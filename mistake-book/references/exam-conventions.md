# 中国初中考试惯例 / Chinese junior-high exam conventions

Reference for interpreting exam photos and producing outputs that look like a
real Chinese school worksheet. Applies to 初中 (grades 7–9); the worked example
throughout is **七年级上册 (Grade 7, semester 1)**.

---

## 1. Paper structure

| 题型 | Typical weight | Marking |
|---|---|---|
| 选择题 (multiple choice) | 10–12 items × 3分 | 只看得分，无步骤分 |
| 填空题 (fill in the blank) | 6–8 items × 3分 | 填结果，无步骤分 |
| 计算题 (calculate) | 4 items × 5–6分 | **有步骤分** |
| 解答题 / 应用题 (show work) | 3–4 items × 8–10分 | **有步骤分** |
| 探究/综合题 (extension) | 1–2 items × 10–12分 | **有步骤分** |

Papers almost always total **100 分** (or 120 分) and carry a 卷面/书写 deduction
(usually −1 to −3) for untidy handwriting or missing units.

> **Critical for error analysis:** on 计算题/解答题 a student often loses only
> *part* of the marks. Record the partial score (`得 3/6 分`), not just "wrong" —
> a careless slip at the last step is a different problem from a wrong method.

## 2. Standard headings a worksheet should carry

```
七年级上册 数学 错题练习卷
班级：__________  姓名：__________  日期：__________  用时：______
```

Chinese worksheets put the *subject*, *semester*, and *paper type* on line 1, and
blank class/name/date fields on line 2. Answers are never shown on the practice
sheet — only in the separate 答案 output.

## 3. Deduction points that recur constantly (审查重点)

These are the things a Chinese teacher actually marks wrong. Check every
extracted mistake against this list before classifying it:

1. **过程不规范** — no `解：`, no `原式=`, arrow-chained equalities.
2. **单位缺失** — `3` instead of `3 米`, `−5` instead of `−5℃`.
3. **结果未化最简** — fractions not reduced (`6/8` not `3/4`), answers not in
   lowest terms, `±` omitted when a square root has two solutions.
4. **符号错误** — dropped or flipped `−`, especially after 去括号 / 移项.
5. **漏解 / 多解** — 分类讨论 omitted (absolute values, 数轴 distance).
6. **抄错数字** — transcription slips from the previous line.
7. **答题不完整** — no concluding sentence (答：……) on 应用题.
8. **书写不规范** — geometry figures unlabelled, no 直角符号, no 单位 on axes.

## 4. 七年级上册 数学 knowledge map

Use this to attach each mistake to a topic, which drives the variant questions.

| 模块 | 核心考点 |
|---|---|
| 第一章 有理数 | 正负数意义、数轴、相反数、**绝对值**、有理数四则运算、乘方、科学记数法、近似数与有效数字 |
| 第二章 整式的加减 | 单项式/多项式的次数与系数、**同类项**、合并同类项、去括号法则、化简求值 |
| 第三章 一元一次方程 | 等式性质、**移项**与去分母、解方程步骤、列方程解应用题（行程、工程、利润、配套） |
| 第四章 几何图形初步 | 立体图形展开图、直线/射线/线段、**线段中点**、角的度量与换算、余角补角、角平分线 |

Most 七上 mistakes cluster in three places: **绝对值的分类讨论**,
**去括号时的符号**, and **移项时忘记变号**. Check these first.

## 5. Other subjects (when photos are not maths)

| Subject | Common error shape | Variant question style |
|---|---|---|
| 语文 | 文言实词释义、古诗默写错字、阅读理解答题要点不全 | 同篇古文的另一句翻译；同主题阅读题 |
| 英语 | 时态/第三人称单数、介词搭配、单词拼写 | 同语法点的句子改写、填空 |
| 历史 / 道德与法治 | 时间线混淆、概念张冠李戴 | 同一历史事件的另一种问法 |
| 地理 / 生物 | 图表读图错误、术语不准确 | 同类图表判读（图要重画） |

For non-maths subjects, still produce variant questions — a re-worded recall
question is the correct 变式, not a copy of the original.

## 6. Variant (变式) question design rules

A good 变式题 is **not** the original question reworded. Change one thing and
keep the rest, so the student must re-apply the same reasoning:

- **Numeric change** — new values, same structure. (Baseline: always include one.)
- **Structural change** — reverse the unknown (`已知结果求条件`).
- **Context change** — same maths in a new real-world story.
- **Trap-restoring change** — reintroduce the *exact* trap that caught the
  student (e.g. keep the minus sign inside the brackets).

Include at least one *trap-restoring* variant per mistake; that is the one that
actually tests whether the error was fixed.

## 7. Tone for the report

Write in 中文, addressed to the **parent**, about the child in the third person
(`孩子`, `她`). Be specific and non-judgemental — name the *habit*, not the
character (`去括号时容易漏掉负号`, not `粗心`). Chinese parents expect
actionable 建议 and a concrete 复习计划, not reassurance.
